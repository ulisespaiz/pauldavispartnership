Offices and Residence

Completed in 2000, this 4,608 SF mixed-use building includes 2,932 SF of commercial office space with 1,675 SF private residence above.

Careful study and use of the site allowed for maximum square footage on a small building lot.

An underground parking garage keeps parking on site.

Construction types include a post-tension slab over parking.

Future planning includes an elevator for aging residents.

Materials utilized include use of stucco, concrete roof tiles & wood trellis accents.

Landscaping with native plants requiring minimal irrigation.

Award: City of Monterey 2002 Award of Merit, Mixed-Use Category
