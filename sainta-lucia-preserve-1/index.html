Santa Lucia Preserve 1

8,400 SF home located in a private reserve in Carmel Valley.

Home was set into the side of a hill to capture beautiful open space views on different levels created for various types of living/entertaining arrangements. Project included main residence, guest house, caretaker’s cottage and barn.

Richard M. McPherson Landscape Architecture

General Contractor: Santa Lucia Builders

Interior Design: Andrea Bartholick Pace

AQUA magazine, April 2009 “Pristine Preserve”

Paul Davis Partnership is a premier architectural firm specializing in sustainable design and innovative building solutions. We create spaces that inspire and endure.
