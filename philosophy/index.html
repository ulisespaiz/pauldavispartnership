PHILOSOPHY

We are your neighbors.

We believe that architecture is more than the design of buildings. Architecture is the very infrastructure that creates community. By leading a creative and collaborative process that results in functional, sustainable and unique projects, we serve both our clients and community by meeting their needs and enhancing the site and its surroundings.

Because we are about community, our philosophy is to focus on many different types of projects including civic structures, urban design studies, churches, commercial/office buildings, hotel resorts, mixed-use buildings, commercial/office projects, multi-family communities and custom residential.

As professionals and citizens, we are compelled to participate in our community and professional organizations. Both principals have served on boards and committees for the American Institute of Architects, Cities of Monterey, Seaside, Marina, and Sand City and for a variety of charitable organizations.

We are honored to have the confidence of our clients and community. We pledge to utilize our skills as architects and inspire the creation of private and public projects that enhance our surroundings and the environment.
