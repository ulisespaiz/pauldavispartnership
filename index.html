About Us

Monterey Bay's Premier Architectural Design Studio Since 1984

Read more

At The Paul Davis Partnership, we believe architecture transcends mere building design — it forms the very infrastructure of community. By leading a creative and collaborative process, we deliver functional, sustainable, and distinctive projects.

Our commitment to diversity in architecture sees us engaging in a variety of projects, from civic structures and urban design to churches, commercial buildings, hotel resorts, mixed-use developments, and custom residences.

As dedicated professionals and active citizens, our principals contribute to both local and professional communities, serving on boards for the American Institute of Architects and various cities and charitable organizations in the Monterey area.

Trusted by our clients and cherished by our community, we pledge to leverage our architectural expertise to inspire and realize both private and public projects that enrich our environment and community life.

Our Mission

Functionality, sustainability & aesthetics

At The Paul Davis Partnership, we are dedicated to designing spaces that reflect the highest standards of functionality, sustainability, and aesthetics. We strive to create architecture that integrates seamlessly with its environment and inspires the people who interact with it.

Our Values

Innovation & responsibility

Innovation: We continually push the boundaries of design, integrating cutting-edge technologies and methods into our work.

Sustainability: We are committed to creating designs that are environmentally responsible and resource-efficient, from planning to demolition.

Philosophy

Design that endures

Great architecture is not about trends — it is about understanding place, purpose, and people. Every project we take on begins with listening: to our clients, to the site, to the community. That attentiveness shapes every line we draw.

We hold ourselves accountable to both aesthetic ambition and practical integrity. A building should look beautiful on day one and perform beautifully for decades.
