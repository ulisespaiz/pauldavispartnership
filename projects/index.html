  • Holman Ranch, Carmel Valley

    Restoration of 6,000 SF hacienda originally built in 1928.

    Paul W. Davis and Stocker & Allaire General Contractors worked together to draft a restoration plan for the 6,000-square-foot hacienda originally designed for San Francisco businessman Gordon Armsby in 1928 to bring its design back to that era.

  • 286 Eldorado Street

    Offices and Residence
    286 Eldorado Street

    Completed in 2000, this 4,608 SF mixed-use building includes 2,932 SF of commercial office space with 1,675 SF private residence above.

    Careful study and use of the site allowed for maximum square footage on a small building lot.

    An underground parking garage keeps parking on site.

    Construction types include a post-tension slab over parking

  • Castro Plaza

    Completed 2007
    The 16,000 SF project includes a new library, child and family educational center, historical exhibits, community meeting facilities and county offices. The plaza originally planned for the town by Juan Castro was restored, complete with a fountain from Castroville’s sister city Guanajuato in Mexico.

  • Sainta Lucia Preserve 1

    8,400 SF home located in a private reserve in Carmel Valley.

    Home was set into the side of a hill to capture beautiful open space views on different levels created for various types of living/entertaining arrangements. Project included main residence, guest house, caretaker’s cottage and barn.

    Richard M. McPherson Landscape Architecture

    General Contractor: Santa Lucia Builders

    Interior Design: Andrea Bartholick Pace
