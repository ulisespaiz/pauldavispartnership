About the project

The Alisal Integrated Health Center is a two-story clinic on North Sanborn Road in East Salinas that brings Monterey County behavioral health and medical services together in one building. It includes counseling and therapy offices, exam and treatment rooms, observation spaces and a multipurpose room for community gatherings. Services are offered in English and Spanish, with a special focus on teenagers and young adults facing serious mental health and substance use challenges. Ground was broken in March 2022, and construction was completed in 2024.
