Oak Park Community, Paso Robles

Year Completed 2020
Location City of Paso Robles, County of San Luis Obispo
Units Over 300 affordable housing units (four-phase project)

Oak Park Community Apartments located in the City of Paso Robles, County of San Luis Obispo.

Completed in 2020, this affordable multifamily housing community consists of a four-phase project totaling over 300 affordable housing units, that replaced the 148 units of deteriorated public housing previously existing on the site.
