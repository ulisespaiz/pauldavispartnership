Santa Lucia Preserve

Size 8,400 SF
Location Carmel Valley, CA
Contractor Santa Lucia Builders
Landscape Richard M. McPherson Landscape Architecture
Interior Design Andrea Bartholick Pace
Press AQUA magazine, April 2009 — "Pristine Preserve"

8,400 SF home located in a private reserve in Carmel Valley.

Home was set into the side of a hill to capture beautiful open space views on different levels created for various types of living/entertaining arrangements.

Project included main residence, guest house, caretaker's cottage and barn.
