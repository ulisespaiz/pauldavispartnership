Oak Park Community - Paso Robles

  • Completed in 2020
  • Oak Park Community Apartments located in the City of Paso Robles, County of San Luis Obispo
  • Affordable multifamily housing community consisting of a four-phase project totaling over 300 affordable housing units that replaced the 148 units of deteriorated public housing previously existing on the site.

More about this project

Oak Park began as housing built in 1941 for Camp Roberts personnel, and the Paso Robles Housing Authority took it over in 1953. By the 2010s the buildings were beyond repair, with tree roots in the plumbing and asbestos, so the Authority chose to rebuild. The first phase broke ground in February 2013, and the final phase, Oak Park 4, opened in 2020 with 75 apartments, a 4,172 SF resident center, sports courts, a tot lot and solar power. Oak Park 4 received the Paso Robles Chamber of Commerce 2019 Beautification of the Year Award.
