Castro Plaza

Completed 2007, the 16,000 SF project includes a new library, child and family educational center, historical exhibits, community meeting facilities and county offices.

The plaza originally planned for the town by Juan Castro was restored, complete with a fountain from Castroville's sister city Guanajuato in Mexico.
